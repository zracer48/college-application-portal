2026–2027 SAT & ACT Dates, Deadlines & Fees

U.S. national weekend testing dates and registration costs for the 2026–2027 academic year. Fees shown are standard domestic registration costs, before optional services, and may change. Confirm your selected test date, location, deadlines, and final charges with the testing provider before paying.

Standard SAT: $68. Late registration adds $38, for a $106 total. The SAT does not have a separate essay or science add-on.

ACT — 2026–2027

Standard ACT (English, Math & Reading): $70. Late registration adds $42, for a $112 total. Optional Science: +$5; optional Writing: +$25 (both: +$30). The optional sections cost extra whether registering on time or late.
